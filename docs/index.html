How we decide

The method is public, including where it is weak.

  1. We collect from public ATS APIs. Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters publish their job boards as JSON. These are official endpoints, not scraping.
  2. We do not trust the posting date the ATS reports. When a company reuses a requisition the date inflates to years; sometimes it resets instead. In one case the ATS reported posted 3 days ago for a posting the archive shows existing 427 days earlier.
  3. A third-party archive proves existence. If the Wayback Machine captured that exact posting URL, it existed on that date — that is not falsifiable. We check the HTTP status of each capture so a 404 snapshot is never mistaken for evidence.
  4. Evergreen posts are filtered out. "General Application" and "Talent Community" listings are not trying to fill a specific seat, so they are not signal.
  5. Roles a freelancer cannot take are removed. A sales or support req stuck for 750 days does not convert into contract work. 457 leads were dropped on this rule in the latest run.
  6. Weak evidence does not get promoted. Every lead shows its confidence, and below 50% we never assign Hot.

What this does not do

Stated up front, because a tool that hides its limits is not worth trusting.

  • Archive coverage is roughly 37%. The rest carries no external proof and is shown at lower confidence. No evidence does not mean "new posting".
  • Recognition bias. Better-known companies are archived more thoroughly, so leads skew toward them.
  • Repost and fill signals need time. The archive bootstraps age, but "closed then reopened" only comes from our own daily snapshots. That is why the Hot count is small this early.
  • Stuck does not mean they will outsource. This gives you a reason to reach out. It does not promise a contract.

If you are the one hiring

We will remove your postings. Open an issue with your company name or job board URL and it goes into seeds/blocklist.json. On the next run the pipeline deletes the accumulated observation history too — we do not just stop collecting and keep what we already have.
