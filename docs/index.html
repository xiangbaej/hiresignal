How we decide

The method is public, including where it is weak.

  1. We collect from public ATS APIs. Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters publish their job boards as JSON. These are official endpoints, not scraping.
  2. We do not trust the posting date the ATS reports. When a company reuses a requisition the date inflates to years; sometimes it resets instead. In one case the ATS reported posted 3 days ago for a posting the archive shows existing 427 days earlier.
  3. A third-party archive proves existence. If the Wayback Machine captured that exact posting URL, it existed on that date — that is not falsifiable. We check the HTTP status of each capture so a 404 snapshot is never mistaken for evidence.
  4. Evergreen posts are filtered out. "General Application" and "Talent Community" listings are not trying to fill a specific seat, so they are not signal.
  5. Roles a freelancer cannot take are removed. A sales or support req stuck for 750 days does not convert into contract work. 471 leads were dropped on this rule in the latest run.
  6. Weak evidence does not get promoted. Hot requires at least three independent signals to agree, and we drop it for anything older than 730 days. Today that leaves 8 Hot out of 451 — the label is meant to be rare. Each card also shows what share of our signals we could actually evaluate; that number is coverage, not a probability.

What this does not do

Stated up front, because a tool that hides its limits is not worth trusting.

  • Archive evidence is a filter, not a score. We only surface postings the Wayback Machine actually captured, which is roughly 37% of what we observe. That is why every lead here carries proof — and also why this list is a filtered slice, not a market census. Absence from the archive does not mean a posting is new; it means we cannot prove anything about it, so we stay quiet.
  • That filter causes recognition bias. The archive keeps better-known companies more thoroughly, so the list skews toward companies people already talk about. Today it leans heavily to VC-funded AI and developer-tools startups. If you sell into non-tech industries, this list is probably not for you yet — say so in your request and we will tell you honestly.
  • Very old postings are demoted, not celebrated. Past 730 days a requisition is more often reused than continuously open, so we stop calling it evidence of a stalled hire and never rank it Hot. A four-year-old "opening" is not a four-year backlog.
  • Repost and fill signals need time. The archive bootstraps age, but "closed then reopened" only comes from our own daily snapshots. That is why the Hot count is small this early.
  • Stuck does not mean they will outsource. This gives you a reason to reach out. It does not promise a contract.

If you are the one hiring

We will remove your postings. Open an issue with your company name or job board URL and it goes into seeds/blocklist.json. On the next run the pipeline deletes the accumulated observation history too — we do not just stop collecting and keep what we already have.
